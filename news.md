# Rescayl

Image upscaling for personal photos, artwork, screenshots, and everyday images.

This preview focuses on image upscaling. Video upscaling and vectorization are planned future capabilities.

Maintained by forsonny, based on Upscayl. The preview is unsigned and uses manual installation with automatic updates disabled.
